# 🚧 sendthistomyfuture.me

You can't talk to your past, but you can send a letter to your future self.

### 🚧 Live project

- [https://sendthistomyfuture.me](https://sendthistomyfuture.me)

### Repository

- [GitHub](https://github.com/Monge1h/sendthistomyfuture.me)

I'm currently working on this project. Sometimes a memory from our past can make for a better day, so with this project you can write down your current thoughts, aspirations and hopes, and receive them in the future as a reminder of who you were.

## Tech stack

**Client:** Next.js, Chakra UI

**Server:** Node, NestJS, MongoDB
